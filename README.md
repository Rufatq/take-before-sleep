# TAKE BEFORE SLEEP

A tactile, rain-soaked late-night corner pharmacy behind a glass night hatch (*Notdienstschalter*), anchored in quiet 3 a.m. human warmth before fracturing into deeply grounded psychological dread.

- **100% pure-code visuals:** Zero external images, zero diffusion assets, zero raster textures, zero system/web fonts. Every brush stroke, dry-chalk paper skip, gouache highlight, glass condensation bead, and calligraphic letter is synthesized in code.
- **Optical Glass Membrane:** Switch the brass desk lamp between `COUNTER` (illuminates your work while turning the glass into a semi-mirror of your back shelves), `DIM` (reveals the stranger and street outside), and `CURB` (throws warm sodium light across the wet sill outside).
- **Hidden Calibration Lab:** Press `L` or open `?lab=1` for live Vacuum Layer isolation, 4x2 Vacuum Grid, 3-Value Silhouette Squint, ground switches, presets, overdrive scrubbers, and `Copy Diff JSON`.

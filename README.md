# TAKE BEFORE SLEEP

A tactile, rain-soaked late-night corner pharmacy behind a glass night hatch (*Notdienstschalter*), anchored in quiet 3 a.m. human warmth before fracturing into deeply grounded psychological dread.

- **100% pure-code visuals:** Zero external images, zero diffusion assets, zero raster textures, zero system/web fonts. Every brush stroke, dry-chalk paper skip, gouache highlight, glass condensation bead, and calligraphic letter is synthesized in code.
- **Optical Glass Membrane:** Switch the brass desk lamp between `COUNTER` (illuminates your work while turning the glass into a semi-mirror of your back shelves), `DIM` (reveals the stranger and street outside), and `SILL` (casts soft sodium light across the recessed hatch sill and wet glass).
- **Hidden Calibration Lab:** Press `L` or open `?lab=1` for live Vacuum Layer isolation, 4x2 Vacuum Grid, 3-Value Silhouette Squint, ground switches, presets, overdrive scrubbers, and `Copy Diff JSON`.

## Controls
- `Click` (or `1 / 2 / 3`): Switch brass desk lamp (`COUNTER` / `DIM` / `SILL`)
- `Hold Intercom Grille` (or hold `Space`): Listen to the stranger outside
- `Click Steel Drawer` (or `D`): Pull in / push out the interlocking *Durchreiche* pass-through drawer
- `Hold Bottle / Jar / Sachet / Roll` (or hold `P`): Dispense into grooved slate tray
- `Click Brass Grace Tin` (or `G`): Quietly tuck an unbilled extra wrap
- `Drag Paper` (or `F`): Fold the waxed dispensing packet
- `Click Brass Stamp` (or `S`): Press the vermilion time seal
- `Click Top Brass Latch` (or `E`): Pull the corrugated steel shutter down at dawn (`T` or click packet to take Dr. Halvorsen's dawn packet)
- `L` (or `?lab=1`): Toggle Hidden Visual-DNA Calibration Lab
